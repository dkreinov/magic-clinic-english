מרפאת הקסמים · front-end direction · 2026-07

The art was never the problem.
The room around it was.

The art already glows — lantern gold, dragon teal, crystal violet. The UI answers it with mud. Four directions below. Each is a straight :root token swap plus one decorative rule. All four were run through the project's own contrast script: 52 of 52 gated pairs pass. No artwork changes, no markup, no view files, no layout. Hebrew-first RTL stays intact. Scroll — every section repaints the page in its palette, phone at real size.

Four rooms, one story

Two keep the night (she reads at bedtime); two open the curtains. Warm on the left of each pair, cool on the right.

Notes

Notes

  • Why it works. Teal, amber and violet flip from pale-on-dark to deep-on-light, so they read as ink colours — closer to how the art's own outlines behave.
  • Best for. Daytime use and the loudest possible answer to "dull". It is the only option that changes the app's whole mood, not its hue.
  • Trade-off. The artwork's bottom fade-to-transparent mask now dissolves into cream rather than dark. It reads as a watercolour bleed here — pleasant, but look at the phone and judge it yourself.
  • Extra work. Needs <meta name="color-scheme"> → light and theme-color → #fff8ec in index.html. Shadows must go low-alpha warm brown (already in the swap) or cards look dirty.

What none of these touch

Every option above is a value-only change inside the :root block of public/styles.css, plus one #app::before decorative rule. No view file, no markup, no illustration, no layout.

The artwork

All eight .webp assets and icon.svg ship exactly as they are. The style is the brief, not the variable.

Hebrew-first RTL

dir="rtl", right-aligned option pills and the dir="ltr" English story block are untouched — the mockups above render them live.

The background recipe

The four-layer gradient string in body is byte-identical; only the four paint tokens it references change value.

The contrast gate

All 52 pairs in scripts/check-contrast.mjs pass for every option, computed with that script's own luminance math.

Type & spacing

Rubik, the 480px column, the 68px nav, the 48px tap targets, every font size — unchanged. Only --radius moves, by 2–8px.

One test to update

tests/background.test.js pins the four paint tokens as literals. Whichever option wins, those four lines get the new hexes — nothing else in the suite moves.
